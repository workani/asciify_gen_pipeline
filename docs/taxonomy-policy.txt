# Asciify family/subfamily taxonomy

Implementation: src/taxonomy.mjs. Family predicates: src/taxonomy-family.mjs.
FAMILY_RULES is one array of [predicate, family] pairs. First match wins.
Latest policy: Unicode names identify the glyph; matching a word mentioned in
another object's name does not establish membership. Conservative null/unknown
is preferable to contamination. Neither families nor enums are model-authored.

SOURCES
Pinned Unicode 17.0.0 UnicodeData, Blocks, NameAliases, emoji-data and emoji-test
in data/unicode/17.0.0. Manifest records official URLs and SHA-256 hashes, checked
on load. Runtime Script-escape version, data and code enter the taxonomy version.
Corpus labels, aliases and model text are not used for family assignment.

ORDERED IDENTITY RULES
 1 whitespace-control: Cc, Cf, Zs, Zl, Zp.
 2 arrow: a standalone symbol whose head identity is ARROW, ARROWHEAD or HARPOON
   (whole words, including plurals). Exclude combining/letter marks, phonetic
   modifier letters, notation systems, shafts alone, compound math identities,
   and emoji whose official subgroup identifies a weapon/heart/person instead.
 3 emoji: official heart subgroup (except love letter), a standalone heart or
   floral-heart-bullet identity, or one of the eight BLACK/WHITE card suits.
 4 box-drawing: Box Drawing/Block Elements symbols, or BOX DRAWINGS identities.
 5 bullet: standalone typographic list markers, Po/So only. Exact grammatical
   names cover BULLET, WHITE/INVERSE/TRIANGULAR/HYPHEN BULLET and BLACK
   LEFTWARDS/RIGHTWARDS BULLET. Mathematical bullet operators are excluded.
 6 math: unambiguous standalone plus/minus/multiplication/division/equals signs,
   including HEAVY variants in emoji/geometric blocks.
 7 shape: geometric blocks, or a geometric head noun in eligible symbol blocks
   or the official emoji geometric subgroup. Exclude math blocks and identities
   naming letters, numbers, cups, functional symbols, SignWriting or other
   notation. Enclosures and operands do not define the containing object's family.
 8 letter: L*, or an enclosed alphabetic LETTER identity (Latin/Greek/Cyrillic/
   Hebrew/Arabic). LOVE LETTER and combining letter marks are excluded.
 9 digit: Nd/No. No bare DIGIT keyword admission of unrelated symbols.
10 emoji: Extended_Pictographic, excluding copyright/registered/trademark;
   exact official emoji sequences also qualify.
11 currency: Sc.
12 math: Sm or symbols in Mathematical Operators, Supplemental Mathematical
   Operators, Miscellaneous Mathematical Symbols-A/B.
13 punctuation: P*.
14 misc-symbol: remaining S*.
15 unknown: everything else; sentinel, not a new selectable family.

Head noun is the last token after stripping a trailing standalone WITH, POINTING
or POINTED clause. UP-POINTING stays an adjective. This is only one piece of
identity evidence: CUP ON BLACK SQUARE is still a cup, not a square.
No substring matching: CYRILLIC SMALL LETTER NARROW O cannot match ARROW.

SEQUENCES
Variation selectors preserve the base character identity. Other sequences use
exact emoji-test identities; arbitrary concatenations are unknown. The corpus
sequence table overlaps the character table. Counts report rows in each table;
random samples deduplicate encoded glyphs and sample without replacement.

SUBFAMILIES (closed, scoped enums)
arrow: right, left, up, down, diagonal
emoji: face, hand, heart, animal, food, flag, object, card-suit
shape: circle, square, triangle, diamond, star
letter: latin, greek, cyrillic, hebrew, arabic
math: operator, relation, set, logic
punctuation: quote, dash, bracket, terminal, annotation
bullet: dot, dash, arrowhead, checkmark

Letters use Script escapes, with official decomposition/explicitly named Latin
base letters for Common enclosed forms. Mathematical alphanumeric letters use
latin by product convention (including Greek glyphs) plus script_style; original
identities are retained. Other mathematical digits/operators keep their identity.
Emoji use official group/subgroup plus explicit identity exceptions: love letter
and spider web are objects, not heart/animal. Paw prints, pig nose, microbe and
other narrow-enum mismatches remain null. Face emojis with heart eyes remain face.
The eight BLACK/WHITE SPADE/HEART/DIAMOND/CLUB SUIT identities use card-suit
before the general heart rule, consistently across presentation selectors.
Other heart identities keep heart. Editorial reference marks, daggers, asterisks,
asterisms and section/pilcrow signs use punctuation/annotation; mathematical
asterisk operators and ornamental symbol identities retain their own families.
Other subfamilies use Unicode-name regex with identity precedence. Barb position,
shading, decoration and secondary operands do not determine the class. Shape
heads outrank component words; pentagons/hexagons/rectangles are not squares.

GENERATOR CONTRACT
Code-assigned basis is unicode. Coverage nulls have null basis. Known enum gaps
(other scripts, explicitly unsupported shapes, circular/bidirectional arrows,
and punctuation identities such as ampersands, solidi and percent signs)
are quarantined before renders or model calls. They cannot be laundered into a
false model label. Remaining nulls can receive a rendered model choice ONLY from
the family's enum, basis=model, with independent factual review. Unsupported
choices require needs_review and are withheld. Families without an axis and
unknown require null/null. No invented classes or cross-family fallback.
Schema v4 passes fixed taxonomy to both writers, versions cached outputs, and
revalidates family/subfamily/basis/properties at parse and export. Class names
must use exact enum spellings in descriptions and retrieval phrases.
Color, case, ornate, filled, size, script_style and stroke are properties.

COLORS DATASET
Colors is a complete collection, not a family or subfamily. Official emoji-test
swatch identities give 36 fully-qualified glyphs spanning 12 colors: red, orange,
yellow, green, blue, light blue, purple, pink, brown, black, grey, white.
Circles/squares stay shape; solid hearts stay emoji/heart. Hearts supply colors
without a circle/square emoji. No fake cyan square or pink circle is invented.
The collection document carries colors/colours/palette labels and every member;
its contract is to expand the complete set without pruning individual colors.
There are no per-character 'colors' aliases and no arbitrary colored-object
membership. This dataset metadata is not an offline search implementation.
Membership is assigned in code even when the writer omits it. Model-proposed
membership for non-swatches is rejected. The full collection is exported even
when only a subset of character records has been generated. Monochrome BLACK/
WHITE shape names alone do not establish color; explicit text presentation is
excluded from swatch membership.

  node scripts/taxonomy/coverage.mjs --spot
Optional: --ref-db PATH, --out PATH; otherwise GEN_REF_DB / GEN_ASCIIFY_ROOT.
Reports: out/taxonomy/coverage.{txt,json}, family-samples.{txt,json},
colors_collection.json. Complete per-family dumps: .ucd/families/FAMILY.txt.
This file is regenerated from docs/taxonomy-policy.txt plus actual coverage.
All remaining unknowns and nulls are printed; closed enums are not stretched to
inflate coverage. There are only seven bullet identities, so the random sample
shows all seven rather than padding it to ten with unrelated characters.
Live generator testing is user-run only; inspect out/test-results.json afterward.
